\noindent\textit{Budget notes.} The DMIX is the largest noise contributor
(47\,\% of the RX output noise) and the largest IIP3 contributor (46\,\%), so DMIX-14
and DMIX-15 are the tightest numbers in the RX. A double-balanced Gilbert cell with a
source-degenerated $g_m$ stage and resistive + capacitive load fits the budget. A
current-mode passive mixer with a TIA is the fallback if DMIX-15 cannot be met. In that
case the integrator rechecks DMIX-04 and DMIX-09. The conversion gain is held at 8\,dB
because the 710\,mVpp diff.\ output at $-22$\,dBm is the largest swing a 1.1\,V resistive
load can carry. A higher gain lowers the noise but costs compression.
Details behind the short rows: with a tail current source the DMIX must tolerate
$\pm 50$\,mV on its RF gates (DMIX-04); the noise in DMIX-14 includes the image-band
noise folded by the single mixer; the output pole (DMIX-12) attenuates LO and 2LO
feedthrough; IIP3 0.7\,V pk diff.\ is $\approx +4$\,dBm re 100\,$\Omega$ diff.; the
LO leakage limit (DMIX-21) gives $\leq -70$\,dBm at the antenna through LNA-13.

\noindent\textit{Note:} the LPF is assigned to the VGA designer in the design
proposal (decision~7, \dpref{13}); by default in the specification it belongs
to DMIX.
